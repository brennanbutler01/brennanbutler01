# Brennan Butler

Founding software engineer in San Francisco building production AI agents and full-stack products. I take products from idea to release, with ownership across architecture, implementation, testing, and production operations.

## Engineering work

**[Nombo](https://www.nombo.ai/) — formerly Chestnut.** I joined as the first engineering hire and became Founding Software Engineer, continuing through the company's pivot. I set technical direction and took Nombo from an empty repository to a shipping Mac and iPhone AI assistant. My work spans the agent runtime, tool orchestration, memory, evaluations, approval boundaries, background workers, billing, and shared desktop/mobile contracts.

Previously, I built the company's Chestnut Mortgage platform: converting Fannie Mae policy into structured rules, connecting borrower documents to underwriting checks, and automating lender pricing and follow-up. Final underwriting decisions stayed with people. The mortgage product has since been retired.

**State of Oregon.** Across engineering and quality roles, I modernized C# and Oracle PL/SQL applications, resolved recurring application and database failures, and reduced a longstanding support backlog. At the Oregon Health Authority, I led data modeling and source-to-target mapping for a migration. At Forestry and Human Services, I built documentation, reporting, and staff-training tools with nontechnical users.

**[Alpaca Sho Pro](https://www.alpacashopro.com/).** Co-founded and built a platform used by organizers and exhibitors for registration, judging, payments, and automated scorecards at events handling hundreds to thousands of alpacas.
